### Fixed
- **Network share paths work in path remaps** (#2831): a remap that pointed at a Windows share, such as a Calibre push remap of `/downloads/BOOKS:\\192.168.1.4\MEDIA\BOOKS`, was treated as a Linux path, so pushes asked Calibre for `\\192.168.1.4\MEDIA\BOOKS/Author/file.epub` with mixed separators, and the forward slash form `//nas/books` lost its leading slash and stopped being a share at all. Shares now behave like drive letters: `\\server\share` and `//server/share` are both understood on either side of a pair, matched without regard to case, and rebuilt in the separator style you wrote. A share that names a server but no share, like `\\nas`, is now rejected when you save it. For Calibre on Windows a share address is more reliable than a mapped drive letter, which the running Calibre may not be able to see. One thing to check if you already use a remap that starts with two forward slashes: a side like `//data/books` now counts as the share `books` on the server `data`, so it matches without regard to case. A side with a single segment after the slashes, like `//data`, is unchanged.
