### Fixed
- **Pages on phones** (#3052). A round of fixes for screens that broke on a phone:
  - The menu behind the hamburger button now scrolls, so the admin menu no longer runs off the bottom of the screen with its last entries out of reach.
  - Wanted keeps the book title readable: the format picker and the Unmonitor and Search buttons move to a line under the title instead of squeezing it to nothing.
  - Users scrolls sideways when the table is wider than the screen, so Reset password and Delete are no longer cut off.
  - The Calibre rollback and Push all dialogs keep their buttons on screen and scroll their content instead.
  - Logs shows each entry as a short block on a phone, so the message is readable instead of squeezed into a sliver.
  - The bar that appears when you select books or authors stays on one row, with the less common actions under More, so it no longer covers the list and the page buttons.
  - Settings picks a section from a dropdown on a phone, so choosing one shows it right away, and the back button now returns to the section you were on before instead of leaving Settings.
  - The update notice, the Fix match dialog, the search page, the library search results, the filter menu and the download client form all fit narrow screens.
