### Fixed
- **Back button with a dialog open** (#3052). On Android the back gesture or button used to leave the page behind an open dialog, throwing away whatever you had typed into Edit metadata, Edit author or Add to library. Back now closes the dialog and leaves you on the page, one dialog at a time when one is open on top of another, and closing a dialog any other way no longer leaves an extra step in your back history. Every dialog now closes with Escape, keeps Tab inside itself, returns focus to the button that opened it, and is announced as a dialog by screen readers. With a phone in landscape, Reconcile catalogue and Edit author now scroll as a whole instead of squeezing their lists into a sliver, and Edit metadata no longer lets the page behind it scroll.
