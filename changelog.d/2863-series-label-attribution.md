### Fixed
- **A series label no longer hides a release's author** (#2863): a release like "Heart of Glass, Skye Druids (03) by Donna Grant EPUB" was grabbed for Jennifer Hillier's "Heart of Glass", because the author check only read "by <author>" when it came straight after the title. It now also reads it after a series name and number, so a same titled book by someone else is dropped, while the right author, an initial ("J. Hillier"), surname first order, or a coauthor still match. Narrator and translator credits are still ignored, and a release with no author at all is kept as before. The check also now runs on automatic and interactive searches, not only on the fallback path. Thanks dallyboytx for the report and the diagnosis.
