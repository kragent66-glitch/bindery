### Added
- **Duplicates across the whole library** (#2999). Books has a new Review duplicates page that lists every author's duplicate title groups a page at a time, with the same evidence and buttons as an author's window. It finds exactly the groups each author's window would, and a group drops off once it no longer has two included rows. Thanks to foobarbigtime for the request.

### Changed
- **Review duplicates shows its evidence** (#2999). Every row now says whether it has files (ebook or audiobook, with the format), its status, year, language, ISBN, ASIN and series position, and each group says where the rows agree (a shared ISBN, ASIN or series position) or disagree (different series positions, years more than a year apart, different languages). When exactly one row has files it is marked as the one to keep, and if nothing disagrees the group offers to exclude the empty rows in one confirmed click. That offer never includes a row with files, and nothing is excluded until you confirm.
