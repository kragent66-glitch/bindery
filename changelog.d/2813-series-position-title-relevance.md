### Fixed
- **Books stored as "Series 4: Title" now match releases that name only the title** (#2813): a book whose title carries its series and position ahead of a colon, like "Feral Mage 4: The Mining Company Contract", was only ever matched on the whole name or on the series half, so a release called "The Mining Company Contract by Chase Kilgore" was dropped by both automatic and interactive search. The book's own title is now tried as well when the part before the colon ends in a number ("Feral Mage 4", "Book 8", "Discworld #3"), after the stricter readings fail, and it has to be at least two meaningful words, keep its words in order, and not name a different author. An ordinary "Title: Subtitle" is unaffected. Thanks OMegaliterosMalakas for the report.
