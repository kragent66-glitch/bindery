### Fixed
- **More phone fixes** (#3052). On a narrow phone the Queue's buttons now sit on their own line under a download instead of squeezing its error text into a thin column, and the Match to book search fits the screen. Long author names, book titles and series names wrap instead of widening the page or getting cut to a few letters, and the full release or book name shows on Queue and Wanted. Dropdowns no longer draw their text under the arrow on touch screens, and Save buttons in Settings stay inside their card. Things that only showed on hover can now be reached by tapping: the exact time behind "6m ago" in the Queue, what each file naming token means, and why Enrich is unavailable without an ASIN. View toggles, filter chips, the Library tabs, book checkboxes, the History and Indexer actions and Sign out are easier to hit with a finger. In the dark theme, date pickers, dropdown lists and scrollbars are dark too. Failure reasons in History and the Blocklist are easier to read in the light theme. On the phone calendar, tapping a day with releases lists just that day. A tab left open across an upgrade now reloads itself when it opens a page instead of showing an error.
