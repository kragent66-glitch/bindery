### Fixed
- **A release your download client reports as broken is blocklisted instead of grabbed again** (#3024). Since v1.38.0 a failed download is retried six hours after it failed, which suits a failure that was about the network or the indexer. It did not suit one about the release itself: NZBGet or SABnzbd failing it for missing articles, a repair or unpack that could not succeed, or a corrupt or password protected archive. That release failed the same way every time, so it was sent to your client again on every sweep and filled its download folder with copies of the same failed job. Now NZBGet's `FAILURE/PAR`, `FAILURE/UNPACK`, `FAILURE/HEALTH`, `FAILURE/SCAN` and `FAILURE/BAD`, and the matching SABnzbd failure messages, add the release to the Blocklist with a reason such as `downloadFailed: FAILURE/PAR`, and the next search picks a different one. SABnzbd failures about the client itself, such as a full disk or a failed move, and NZBGet's `FAILURE/MOVE`, keep the six hour retry. NZBGet also reports a problem on its own machine, such as a missing unrar or a full disk while unpacking, with the same status as a broken archive, so when three different releases fail the same way within two hours and nothing completes in between, Bindery stops blocklisting for that client, shows a warning on it under Settings, Download Clients, and retries failures as before until the client completes a download. SABnzbd messages are matched in English, so a SABnzbd set to another language keeps the old behaviour. Releases that failed before you upgrade are not blocklisted retroactively; blocklist them from History if they keep coming back. Thanks to Broomfundel for the report and the numbers.
