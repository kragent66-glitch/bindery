### Fixed
- **A release your download client reports as broken is blocklisted instead of grabbed again** (#3024). Since v1.38.0 a failed download is retried six hours after it failed, which suits a failure that was about the network or the indexer. It did not suit one about the release itself: NZBGet or SABnzbd failing it for missing articles, a repair or unpack that could not succeed, or a corrupt or password protected archive. That release failed the same way every time, so it was sent to your client again on every sweep and filled its download folder with copies of the same failed job. Now those failures add the release to the Blocklist with a reason such as `downloadFailed: FAILURE/HEALTH`, and the next search picks a different one. NZBGet's `FAILURE/HEALTH` and `FAILURE/BAD` and the matching SABnzbd failure messages always do. NZBGet also reports problems on its own machine, such as a missing unrar, a full disk while unpacking or a folder it cannot write, as `FAILURE/UNPACK`, `FAILURE/PAR` or `FAILURE/SCAN`, so for those Bindery first reads the job's log in NZBGet: a CRC error or a missing volume blocklists the release, while a sign of a problem on NZBGet's side does not, and shows a warning on the download client under Settings. On NZBGet 24 and later Bindery also asks NZBGet whether it can find UnRAR when it needs to know, and shows an error on the client when it cannot. When the log says nothing and three different releases fail the same step within two hours, Bindery stops blocklisting that step for that client until NZBGet completes it again. Other failures, such as NZBGet's `FAILURE/MOVE` or SABnzbd running out of disk space, keep the six hour retry. SABnzbd messages are matched in English, so a SABnzbd set to another language keeps the old behaviour. Releases that failed before you upgrade are not blocklisted retroactively; blocklist them from History if they keep coming back. Thanks to Broomfundel for the report and the numbers.
