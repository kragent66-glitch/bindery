### Added
- **Push all to Calibre now queues books, and the Calibre tab shows what is waiting, what failed and why** (#2832). Push all puts every book that is not already delivered into the delivery queue instead of sending them itself, so Calibre does not need to be open and a book is never sent twice. A new Delivery queue section shows how many books are waiting, delivered and failed, when the last one arrived and whether Calibre could be reached, with a table of failed books and their errors, **Retry failed**, **Clear waiting**, and **Reset delivery state** for pointing Bindery at a different Calibre library. Each book's page shows whether it is waiting for Calibre, in Calibre or failed.
