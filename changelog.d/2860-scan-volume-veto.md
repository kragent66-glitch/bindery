### Fixed
- **A library scan no longer attaches one volume of a series to another** (#2860): the scan matched untracked files to wanted books by how alike the titles look, and two volumes of a numbered series look almost identical. An untracked `Defiance of the Fall 01` folder was attached to a wanted "Defiance of the Fall 17", which was then marked owned with the wrong file and never searched. The scan now compares volume numbers first, whether they appear in the title or only in the book folder, using the same rule as adding a book since #2810. The file goes to the volume it really is, or waits under Import, In your library, where the other volume is no longer suggested for it. Numbers that are part of a title, like Fahrenheit 451 or Catch-22, part numbers on a split audiobook and track numbers inside a numbered folder are not mistaken for volumes. Thanks magrhino for the report.
